# Post Schedule Recommendation

**Top pick:** Wednesday, 8–9am — Mid-week morning catches engineers before standup; technical posts perform best when people are in "work brain" and will actually read the second paragraph.

**Backup:** Tuesday, 5–6pm — End-of-day Tuesday hits the post-work scroll and gets overnight engagement from APAC/EU.

Note: Times above assume US Eastern or Central timezone. If you're in Europe, shift to 8–9am CET (catches both EU morning and US East Coast pre-work). If you're in APAC, post at 6pm local — that's EU morning and gets the algorithm running before US wakes up.

**Tip:** Post manually rather than through scheduling tools — LinkedIn's algorithm slightly favors native posts. Copy-paste from post.md directly into the LinkedIn composer.

**One more thing:** The defense/OR niche is small but engaged. Tag 1-2 relevant people if you know any OR researchers or defense-tech engineers — not for vanity, but because their comments pull the post into their network and that's where the relevant audience lives.
